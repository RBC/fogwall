# Your activity

Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar to see what you contributed through fogwall over the last 30 days, 90 days
or 12 months, and on the **Permissions** tab, what you may do. The wrench beside your name opens your profile settings:
email addresses, SCM identities, SSH keys and git credentials.

The **Activity** tab shows:

- your pushes, by status
- pull/merge requests and issues you opened, and the state fogwall last saw them in
- pull/merge requests and issues you edited or commented on, each counted once however many requests your CLI sent
- requests that were rejected, by policy or for their content

The chart splits the period into days, weeks or months, and the table breaks it down by repository.

**Overview** shows your last 30 days beside everyone's, and the most active repositories.

It counts only what passed through fogwall. A pull request merged or closed on the provider directly shows the state of
your last request for it through fogwall. Pull request and issue figures appear only if your administrator has enabled
that path.
